ABOUT OUR CLUB

Together We Can Make a Difference

Shaheed Krishan Lal Yuva Club is a youth-oriented community organization dedicated to bringing young people together for social service, sports, community development and positive activities.

Our goal is to encourage unity, teamwork, responsibility and active participation among the youth while contributing towards the development of our local community.

  • Youth Development
  • Social Service
  • Sports & Fitness
  • Community Development
OUR PURPOSE

Mission & Vision

Building a stronger community through unity, service and youth participation.

Our Mission

To create opportunities for young people to participate in meaningful social, cultural and sports activities while developing leadership, teamwork and community responsibility.

Our Vision

To build an active, united and responsible youth community where everyone works together for positive social change and the betterment of society.

Our Values

Unity, service, honesty, teamwork, dedication and respect are the values that guide every activity of SKL Yuva Club.
